- Since August 2026, working on research and outreach for [synecoculture](https://synecoculture.org/learn/about-synecoculture/) (augmented ecosystems) at the [Synecoculture Association](https://synecoculture.org/).
- Volunteering at [UCHISOTO CAFE](https://uchisotocafe.com/) in Kasama, Sakae-ku, Yokohama, where I run experimental [synecoculture](https://synecoculture.org/learn/about-synecoculture/) plots (since July 2025). Together with a landscaper, a florist and local regulars, I take part in building a community through greenery. I build and run my own tools, such as a species identification app using the iNaturalist API and IoT, to digitize how we manage the plots and record the wildlife we find.
- Running [sanpou](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/sanpou/id6749672561), an iOS app I built on my own. It started from a lack of exercise and the realization that ideas come when you step away from your desk. The app is about the happiness of safely forgetting the ideas you run into in the information age, so you can focus on the present.
- Exploring [PKM](/en/topics/pkm/) as a way to master the [magic](/en/2020/01/20/daisuke-site-launched/) of the information age, and recording my practice on this blog.
